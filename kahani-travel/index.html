About Us

A small office in Thamarassery,
a world of stories.

Since 2021, Kahani Travel & Tours has helped families, friends, pilgrims and professionals from Kozhikode and beyond travel well.

Kahani means story, and we chose the name on purpose. We think every trip should be one worth telling: the first sight of Dal Lake at dawn, a houseboat drifting through the Alleppey backwaters, the quiet of the Haram at Fajr, a child's face when the Burj Khalifa lights up. We look after the small, important details so you can enjoy the parts worth remembering.

We started with tickets and visas and grew into a full-service travel house. We plan international holidays and Indian journeys, look after Kerala getaways, guide pilgrims through Umrah, and handle the paperwork that comes before any trip abroad: passports, visa stamping and certificate attestation, through the right offices and done properly.

We are based at Dr KKMK Complex, near Bishop's House in Thamarassery, and you are always welcome to visit. Come in for a chai and a chat, bring your documents, or simply tell us where you've always wanted to go. We'll take care of the rest, one story, one step at a time.

  • Personal, not packagedEvery itinerary is built around you and your budget.
  • Honest & transparentClear pricing, genuine advice and no hidden surprises.
  • With you all the wayFrom the first enquiry until you are home again.
