The garden island of the Maldives

Farms in the middle, reef all around, and only a few guesthouses on a quiet local island.

Arranged by the family

Ways to spend your days

Reef

Snorkelling

The house reef off the tourist beach is home to turtles, reef sharks and bright coral fish.

Beach

Bikini beach

The designated tourist beach, where swimwear is fine; cycle there in minutes.

Boat

Sandbank picnic

A speedboat to a strip of white sand in the middle of the lagoon.

Ocean

Dolphins & mantas

Half-day trips to look for dolphins, mantas and, in season, whale sharks.

Land

Farm walk

See where the Maldives’ papaya and watermelon grow, and buy fruit at the farm.

Wheels

Cycling

Rent a bike at the guesthouse and circle the island at your own pace.

Trip prices and times vary with the season and the weather. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll book the day that suits you.
